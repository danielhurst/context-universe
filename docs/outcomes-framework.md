# Outcome area	Core question
* Speed to insight	Are decisions reached faster?
* Human leverage	Are people doing more high-value work?
* Decision quality	Are outcomes better, safer, more consistent?
* Signal quality	Are we reducing noise and surfacing what matters?
* Habit formation	Do teams reach for GenAI by default?
* Risk & control	Can we audit, explain, and override?
* Economic impact	Is value compounding over time?


Speed to insight
Time to first usable answer · Time to decision · Cycle time reduction

Human leverage
Hours saved per role · Tasks assisted vs manual · Decision throughput per person

Decision quality
Rework rate · Error / correction rate · Confidence or approval delta

Signal quality
% irrelevant outputs rejected · Early signal adoption rate · False positive / noise ratio

Habit formation
Weekly active users · Repeat usage · Manual fallback rate

Risk & control
Human-in-the-loop % · Traceability coverage · Override frequency

Economic impact
Cost avoided (Phase 1) · Value created (Phase 2) · Cost per use case
